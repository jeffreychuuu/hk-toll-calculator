Western Harbour Crossing

Free road alternatives

No free alternative crosses the harbour — the three harbour tunnels are the only road crossings.

Common questions

How much is the Western Harbour Crossing for a private car?
$20 – $60 on weekdays and $20 – $25 on Sundays and public holidays; the peak windows (08:08–10:14, 16:58–18:59) cost the most.
What do taxis and commercial vehicles pay at the Western Harbour Crossing?
A taxi pays a flat $25; goods vehicles, minibuses and buses pay $50, all day.
Is there a free way across the harbour?
No. The three harbour crossings — Cross-Harbour, Eastern and Western — are all tolled; the main page’s comparison shows which is cheapest right now.
