Tai Lam Tunnel

Common questions

How much is the Tai Lam Tunnel for a private car?
$18 – $45 on weekdays and $18 on Sundays and public holidays, split into peak, normal and non-peak periods.
What do taxis and commercial vehicles pay at the Tai Lam Tunnel?
A taxi pays a flat $28; goods vehicles, minibuses and buses pay $43, all day.
Are there free alternatives towards the west New Territories?
Yes: Tuen Mun Road, free all day; the main page compares the fares and live journey times.
