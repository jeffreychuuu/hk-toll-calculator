Shing Mun Tunnels

The Shing Mun Tunnel links Sha Tin to Tsuen Wan, the main road tunnel between the east and west New Territories, charging one flat fare all day. This page lists the toll for every vehicle class and the free road alternatives.

Common questions

How much is the Shing Mun Tunnel for a private car?
A flat $8 all day.
How much do goods vehicles and buses pay at the Shing Mun Tunnel?
Every class pays the same flat $8 — goods vehicles, minibuses and buses alike.
Are there free roads between Sha Tin and Tsuen Wan?
Yes: Lam Kam Road / Castle Peak Road, free all day; the main page compares them with the tunnel.
